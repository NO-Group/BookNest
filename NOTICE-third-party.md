# Third-party assets in BookNest

## Emote artwork (`assets/emotes/`)

The static artwork for BookNest's custom emotes is **Twemoji** — the
Twitter Emoji project, now maintained by the community as
[jdecked/twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) — sourced via the
`emoji-datasource-twitter` packaging.

Graphics License: **CC-BY 4.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>).
Copyright (c) Twitter, Inc and other contributors. The set's license text
ships alongside this notice. The artwork is cropped to the individual
codepoints BookNest uses; no other modification was made.

BookNest's motion layer (glow, shine, entrance and loop animations), the
emote codes/labels, and the hand-drawn fallback painter are BookNest's own
(N.O Group) and are not part of Twemoji.
