# Delete your BookNest account

_BookNest by N.O Group_

## In the app (instant)

1. Open **BookNest** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Tap **Delete my account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

## Without the app

Email **n.ogroup@yahoo.com** from the email or phone number linked to your
account, with the subject **"Delete my BookNest account"** and your
username. We confirm it is you, and delete the account within 30 days
(usually much sooner).

## What is deleted

- Your profile, username and login.
- Books and chapters you wrote, including drafts.
- Posts, comments, reviews, likes, bookmarks, follows and reading stats.
- Messages you sent and your conversations.
- Gem history, blocks and reports you filed.

## What is kept

- **Payment records** (product, amount, date, reference) are kept only as
  long as financial law requires, and then deleted.
- Messages or chat backups that are already on **other people's devices**,
  or in **your own Google Drive**, are not on our servers. Delete those
  backups from your Drive yourself.
